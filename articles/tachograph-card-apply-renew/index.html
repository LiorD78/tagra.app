Tachograph card renewal online – plus lost cards and first applications

Truck Data TechnologyUpdated October 20265 min read

Every professional driver with a digital tachograph needs a driver tachograph card. Here is where to apply, what it costs, how long it lasts, and what to do if it is lost, stolen or about to expire.

Who needs one

Who needs a tachograph card

Every driver of a vehicle fitted with a digital tachograph needs their own driver tachograph card. It records who was driving, for how long, and confirms your identity to the tachograph — you cannot legally drive a vehicle that requires one without it.

From 1 July 2026, this also applies to vans of 2.5–3.5 t, but only in international transport and cabotage — not for vans used purely for domestic journeys. See Tachographs for vans: new obligation from July 2026 for the full detail on who is affected.

Application

How to apply for a driver tachograph card

In Great Britain, the tachograph card is issued by the DVLA. In Northern Ireland, it is issued by DVA Coleraine.

The application form depends on where you live:

  • Great Britain — form D777B/DL
  • Northern Ireland — form D777(NI) for a new card, or D786(NI) for renewal

A first card costs £32. You can apply online or by post, and DVLA aims to issue it within 10 working days of receiving a valid application.

You can apply online through the official service: gov.uk/apply-driver-digital-tachograph-card.

Cost

What a tachograph card costs

  • First card: £32
  • Renewal: £19
  • Replacement for a lost, stolen or damaged card: £19
  • Malfunctioning card: free – you must return the faulty card
  • Change of name or address: free
  • Exchanging an EU/EEA card for a GB card: £32
  • Card expired more than 5 years ago: treated as a first application, £32
Validity

How to renew your tacho card online, by phone or by post

A tachograph card is valid for 5 years, and DVLA sends a reminder before it expires. There are three ways to renew it:

  • Online – if your card expires within the next 90 days, at gov.uk/apply-driver-digital-tachograph-card. This is the quickest route.
  • By phone – if you hold a GB driving licence and your details have not changed, call DVLA on 0300 790 6109 (Monday to Friday 8am–7pm, Saturday 8am–2pm). Only Visa, Mastercard and Delta are accepted.
  • By post – with form D777B/DL, sent to DVLA, Swansea.

Renewal costs £19. Apply at least 15 working days before your card expires. Do not send your current card with the application – keep using it until the new one arrives.

Northern Ireland: DVA Coleraine sends you a renewal form D786(NI) before your card expires; otherwise use form D777(NI).

Expired card = you cannot drive

If your card has expired and the new one has not arrived, you cannot drive a vehicle that needs a tachograph. The 15-day printout rule only applies to lost, stolen, damaged or malfunctioning cards.

Delivery

How long does a tacho card take to arrive?

DVLA says first, renewed and replacement driver cards are issued within 10 working days of receiving a valid application – longer if it needs to check your details. Applying online is quicker than applying by post.

In Northern Ireland, DVA aims to deliver renewed cards within four weeks, and replacements for lost or stolen cards are normally issued within five working days.

Lost or stolen

Lost or stolen digi card: can you still drive?

Yes – for a limited time. If your card is lost or stolen, report it to DVLA straight away and apply for a replacement within 7 calendar days – online, by phone on 0300 790 6109 or by post. A replacement costs £19 and is issued within 10 working days.

While you wait, you may drive for a maximum of 15 days without the card. Take a printout from the vehicle unit at the start and end of each day (and whenever you change vehicle), and write your name or driver card or licence number, any manual entries and your signature on it.

A forgotten card does not count – you cannot use printouts instead of a card you left at home. If you find a card you reported lost, do not use it: return it to DVLA, Swansea, SA99 1ST.

Damaged or malfunctioning card

Replacing a damaged card costs £19. A malfunctioning card is replaced free, but you must apply by post and return the card – otherwise DVLA treats it as lost and charges the fee. Try the card in another vehicle unit first, and download the data from it before you send it back.

Changes

Changing the name or address on your tacho card

Address: if you hold a GB driving licence, you only need to update the address on your licence. It is free, your tachograph record is updated automatically and no new card is issued.

Name: change the name on your driving licence first, then send form D777B/DL to DVLA, Swansea, SA99 1ST. It is also free, and you keep using your current card until the new one arrives.

Card data

What happens to the data on your card

By law, a tachograph card must be able to store at least 28 days of activity — that is a legal minimum capacity, not a fixed limit. In practice, a card keeps recording until its memory is full, which for an average driver can mean several months of history. Once it is full, the oldest records are overwritten first.

Regardless of how much space is actually left, you must download the data every 28 days.

In Great Britain you must keep an expired card for 56 days (28 days in Northern Ireland) in case it is needed at a roadside check, and you must download the data from it before disposing of it.

Without your own archive of downloaded data, you have no way to prove your driving times and rest periods after the fact.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I apply for a tachograph card?

In Great Britain, apply to the DVLA online or by post using form D777B/DL. In Northern Ireland, apply to DVA Coleraine using form D777(NI). A first card costs £32. Apply online at gov.uk/apply-driver-digital-tachograph-card.

How do I renew my tachograph card?

Online if your card expires within 90 days, by phone on 0300 790 6109 (GB licence holders whose details have not changed) or by post with form D777B/DL. It costs £19. Apply at least 15 working days before expiry – you cannot drive once your card has expired.

What does a tachograph card cost?

A first card costs £32. Renewal costs £19, and so does a replacement for a lost, stolen or damaged card. A malfunctioning card is replaced free if you return it, and changing your name or address is free. Exchanging an EU/EEA card for a GB card costs £32.

What do I do if I lose my tachograph card?

Report it to DVLA straight away and apply for a replacement within 7 calendar days – online, by phone or by post. You may drive for up to 15 days using a printout at the start and end of each day, with your name or card number and your signature on it. A forgotten card does not count.

How long does a replacement tachograph card take?

DVLA issues replacement cards within 10 working days of receiving a valid application. In Northern Ireland, DVA normally issues replacements within five working days.

Is a tachograph card the same as a company card?

No. The driver tachograph card belongs to you personally and identifies you to the tachograph. A separate company card is used by a transport operator to download data from the vehicle unit, and is not a substitute for a driver card. If you run your own vehicles, TAGRA can help with that side of the evaluation too.

I don't live in the UK – where do I apply for a tachograph card?

In the country where you normally live – the card is always issued there. In Ireland the Road Safety Authority (RSA) issues cards (€45 online, €60 by post); in Malta, Transport Malta (paper form DRV 035). Other countries are in the table above – for example the driving licence office in Germany, ÖAMTC or ARBÖ in Austria, ChronoServices in France and Kiwa Register in the Netherlands.

Can I renew my tacho card by phone?

Yes, if you hold a GB driving licence and your details have not changed. Call DVLA on 0300 790 6109, Monday to Friday 8am–7pm or Saturday 8am–2pm. Only Visa, Mastercard and Delta are accepted.

How long does a new tacho card take to arrive?

DVLA issues first, renewed and replacement cards within 10 working days of a valid application; applying online is quicker than by post. In Northern Ireland, DVA aims to deliver renewed cards within four weeks.

Can I drive if my card has expired and the new one hasn't arrived?

No. You cannot drive a vehicle that needs a tachograph with an expired card – the 15-day printout rule is only for lost, stolen, damaged or malfunctioning cards. Apply at least 15 working days before expiry.

How do I change the address on my tacho card?

GB licence holders only need to update the address on their driving licence. It is free, the tachograph record updates automatically and no new card is issued. For a name change, update your licence first, then send form D777B/DL to DVLA.

Where is my tachograph card number?

On the back of the card, in field 5b (“Digital tachograph driver card number”). Field 5a is your driving licence number.
