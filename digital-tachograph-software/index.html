Legal deadlines

Know your tachograph download obligations

Under Regulation (EU) No 581/2010, driver card data must be downloaded at least every 28 days and vehicle unit data at least every 90 days. Keep the data available for inspections. Since 1 July 2026, tachographs are also required in vans over 2.5 t used in international transport of goods and cabotage.

FAQ

Questions about TAGRA tachograph software

Is TAGRA free tachograph software?
TAGRA III offers a 30-day free trial with full functionality and no credit card required. After the trial, you need a paid licence to continue using it.
Which card reader do I need?
You need a USB driver card reader to read your driver card with TAGRA. A USB driver card reader is included free when you purchase a TAGRA TRUCKER licence, with free delivery to all EU countries.
Does TAGRA read .DDD files?
Yes. TAGRA reads driver card files in .DDD format from a USB card reader and also works with vehicle unit files.
Does TAGRA run on Mac?
Only Windows 10 and Windows 11 are supported. TAGRA III does not have a supported native Mac version.
How often must I download my driver card?
Driver card data must be downloaded at least every 28 days under Regulation (EU) No 581/2010. The downloaded data must be kept available for inspections.
What is the difference between a company card and a driver card?
A driver card holds the individual driver's tachograph activity data and is read using a driver card reader. A company card is used when downloading vehicle unit mass memory with a device such as DOWNLOAD BOX II S; it does not replace the driver card.
